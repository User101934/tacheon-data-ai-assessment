# Task 1 - Product Scoping

## Overview

This task focuses on scoping an internal marketing insights tool for a marketing technology company.

The goal of the tool is to help marketing analysts and account managers quickly answer the question:

> "How is our marketing performing across channels right now, and where should we focus?"

Currently, answering this question requires manually collecting data from multiple tools, combining reports, and creating summaries manually. This process is slow, inconsistent, and heavily dependent on individual team members.

The proposed solution focuses on improving operational efficiency and reporting consistency while fitting into the company's existing workflow and tools.

---

# Problem Statement

Marketing teams currently:
- Pull data manually from multiple platforms
- Spend significant time combining reports
- Generate inconsistent insights depending on the analyst
- Lack a centralized performance overview

This creates delays in reporting and reduces scalability.

---

# Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is a centralized internal dashboard that:
- Aggregates marketing data from existing platforms
- Standardizes KPIs across channels
- Surfaces performance trends and insights
- Helps analysts quickly identify areas requiring attention

The tool is intentionally designed to work alongside existing marketing tools rather than replace them.

---

# Primary Users

## Primary Users
- Marketing Analysts

## Secondary Users
- Account Managers

The first version is focused primarily on internal operational efficiency.

---

# V1 Scope

The first version includes:

- Unified performance dashboard
- KPI overview
- Weekly trend monitoring
- Best and worst performing channel insights
- Recommendation summary
- Basic filtering options

The goal of v1 is to provide fast, consistent, and actionable performance visibility.

---

# Out of Scope

The following features were intentionally excluded from v1:

- Predictive analytics
- AI-generated recommendations
- Campaign editing
- Real-time streaming analytics
- Automated budget optimization
- Advanced machine learning models

These were excluded to keep the solution simple, reliable, and easier to adopt initially.

---

# Key Product Decisions

## Why a Dashboard-Based Approach?

A dashboard provides:
- Faster access to insights
- Easier comparison across channels
- Lower learning curve for internal users
- Standardized reporting experience

---

## Why Focus on Internal Users First?

Internal analysts are the primary users responsible for generating insights and responding to client requests.

Improving internal efficiency first creates a stronger operational foundation before expanding to client-facing functionality.

---
